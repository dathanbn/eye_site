Voice and captions

Warm, plain English, confident

On the graphic

"You" for the patient, "we" for the practice, "Dr. Jones" for doctors. Plain word first, medical term second: cloudy lens (cataract). Sentence case; one italic word per headline, usually the last. No hype, no medical promises, no exclamation points, no emoji.

Every caption, four parts

1. First line says what it is (the only line visible before "more"). 2. One or two sentences on why it matters to patients. 3. A light call to action: call, visit the link, or comment. 4. Three to five hashtags, the same core set: #EyeSiteSacramento #SacramentoEyeDoctor #EastSacramento.

Before anything goes out

No patients, names, schedules or screens in frame. Staff shown have said yes. Awards use the exact name, organization and year from the source. "Board certified" only with the full board name. The Kings line only in wording Dr. Rashidi confirmed. Administration's approval is in the sheet.
