# Insurance

The six plan logos the website shows in its insurance row, taken from the old eyesitemd.com at 400px wide: Medicare, Sutter Health, Hill Physicians, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Secure Horizons by UnitedHealthcare, Health Net. They belong to their respective plans and are shown only to state that the practice accepts them.

On the site each sits in a white card with a hairline border, 84px tall, scaled to 44px high, in full color with no filter. Six across on desktop, three on tablet, two on phones. Add a plan by adding a card; remove one by removing it. Never recolor or crop a plan's logo.
