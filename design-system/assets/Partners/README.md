# Partners

`kings-sac-white.png` is the Sacramento Kings "SAC" crest as a one-color white cutout, 246 × 272, used only in the Kings strip on the homepage trust panel. It was made from a 520px raster of the team's crest: the white background was removed, the purple crown and band became solid white, the gray ball a step dimmer, and the letters and seams were knocked out so the navy shows through.

The crest is the team's trademark. Use it only in the Kings strip, only on navy, never recolored further, cropped or paired with other wording. Before launch, get the team's written approval for this one-color version or replace it with an approved file, ideally a vector.
