# Social

One visual kit serves Instagram, Facebook, Google Business Profile and LinkedIn, so every post is recognizably Eye Site before the logo is read.

## Formats

| Format | Size | Where it runs |
|---|---|---|
| Square | 1080 × 1080 | Instagram feed, Facebook, LinkedIn |
| Portrait | 1080 × 1350 | Instagram feed (more screen on mobile) |
| Story / Reel cover | 1080 × 1920 | Instagram and Facebook stories |
| Landscape | 1200 × 628 | Facebook link posts, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile updates |

Safe margins: `space-24` (96px) on every side of a square, portrait or landscape post; 250px top and bottom on a story so platform chrome never covers text.

## Templates

- **Education** on `canvas`: an amber rule (48 × 6px, `amber`) above an `eyebrow` in `blue` such as EYE HEALTH, an `h1`-style serif headline up to three lines at 72px in `ink`, body in `slate` at 36 to 40px, the logo lockup bottom-left, the website bottom-right in `blue`. Use for tips, condition explainers and FAQ answers.
- **Announcement** on `navy`: the same structure with the headline in `on-navy`, body in `on-navy-soft`, the amber rule kept, and the logo on a `canvas` tile with `radius-md`. Use for hours, new services, holiday closures.
- **Photo**: a full-bleed photograph with a `navy` overlay at 50%, headline in `on-navy`, the amber rule, logo on a white tile. Use for team news, awards, office photos.
- **Review** on `ice`: a large serif opening quote mark in `sky`, the review in `lead` scaled to 44px in `ink`, the reviewer's first name and city in `caption` style at 32px in `mute`, logo bottom-left. Quote real reviews with permission.

## Rules

- Text never below 36px at 1080px wide. Headlines in the serif, everything else in the sans.
- One amber element per post: the rule, or a highlighted word, never both.
- The logo appears exactly once, at least 180px wide, with `space-6` clear space.
- No gradients, no drop shadows on text, no emoji in the graphic. Captions may carry one emoji.
- Captions open with the point, name a doctor or office where relevant, and end with the phone number of the office the post is about. Hashtags go on the last line and stay under five.
- Google Business Profile posts use the landscape template with the call to action set to the website or the Sacramento phone number.
- Export at 2x for Instagram and LinkedIn (2160 × 2160 for a square) as PNG; Facebook and Google accept 1x JPEG at quality 90.
