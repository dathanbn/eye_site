Sina Rashidi, M.D.

Fellowship-trained in cornea and refractive surgery, Dr. Rashidi brings corneal transplantation, LASIK and PRK, and minimally invasive glaucoma surgery to the practice.

He offers advanced diagnosis and treatment of glaucoma, including the latest laser and surgical therapies and both traditional and minimally invasive glaucoma surgery. His cornea fellowship allows him to manage complex corneal disease, perform corneal transplants and provide vision correction with LASIK and PRK.
