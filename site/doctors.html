Richard A. Jones, M.D.

Dr. Jones has practiced ophthalmology in Sacramento since 1987 and has held nearly every leadership role the region's medical community offers.

He graduated from the University of California, Davis with highest honors and earned his medical degree from the University of Texas Medical Branch, where he was elected to Alpha Omega Alpha, the medical honor society, in the top 1% of his class. After a general surgery internship at the University of Arizona he completed his ophthalmology residency at the University of Texas.

In Sacramento he has served as Chief of the Department of Ophthalmology at both Mercy General and Sutter General Hospitals and as Medical Director of Capital City Surgery Center, one of the busiest eye surgery centers in Northern California. He has been program director and president of the Alta Ophthalmologic Society, is a fellow of the American Academy of Ophthalmology, and belongs to the American Society of Cataract and Refractive Surgery, the California Academy of Ophthalmology, the California Medical Association and the American Medical Association.

Dr. Jones is a past president of the Sierra Sacramento Valley Medical Society and serves as a delegate to the California Medical Association, helping shape health policy for the state. He has been an associate medical director for Vision Service Plan, has volunteered overseas with the International Eye Foundation, and takes part in the medical society's SPIRIT program, restoring sight to uninsured patients at no cost. He is honored to have worked alongside his predecessors Drs. William Kohl, Norman Schwilk, Randal Childers and Richard Zipf.

An avid cyclist, Dr. Jones has ridden stages of the Tour de France route several times. He also enjoys international travel, hiking, boating and skiing.

Christian L. Serdahl, M.D.

A physician-scientist by training, Dr. Serdahl has published in the leading ophthalmology journals and has led the Sacramento medical community for more than three decades.

Dr. Serdahl holds bachelor's and master's degrees in chemistry from the University of California, Santa Cruz, graduated from the University of Southern California School of Medicine, completed his internship at Los Angeles County Hospital and his ophthalmology residency at UC Davis Medical Center, and began private practice in Sacramento in 1991.

Since then he has held numerous offices in the Sacramento medical community: Chief of Ophthalmology at both Mercy General and Sutter Hospitals, President of the Alta Ophthalmologic Society, Medical Director of Vision Service Plan, President of the Board of Communicare Health Centers, Clinical Professor of Ophthalmology at UC Davis Medical Center, Editor of Sacramento Medicine, President of Sight Savers International, and a peer reviewer for the Medical Board of California.

He has written and lectured on sports-related eye injuries, surgical correction of presbyopia, new techniques in cataract surgery and medical-legal pitfalls in eye care. With his wife and colleague Clarissa Tendero, M.D., he has performed free cataract surgery on medical missions to Mexico, the Philippines and Fiji. Outside medicine he skis, windsurfs, backpacks, fishes and cooks.

David T. Kira, M.D.

Dr. Kira specializes in refractive cataract surgery, maximizing glasses-free vision after surgery, and teaches the next generation of surgeons at UC Davis.

He completed his undergraduate degree in biochemistry and cell biology at the University of California, San Diego, medical school at the University of Pittsburgh, an internship at Scripps Mercy Hospital in San Diego and his ophthalmology residency at UC Davis. He practiced in Davis and Roseville for 14 years with Medical Vision Technology before joining Eye Site Sacramento.

Dr. Kira serves as a Clinical Instructor of Ophthalmology at UC Davis Health in Sacramento, teaching medical and surgical ophthalmology to residents in training. He continues to serve populations in need through no-cost clinics for children and seniors, and seeks opportunities at home and abroad to bring eye care to underserved communities.

He has extensive experience with the latest treatment options, including laser-assisted, blade-free, small-incision cataract surgery with correction of astigmatism and presbyopia, and was the first physician in Placer County to introduce the technique. He also manages glaucoma with lasers, surgery and the newer minimally invasive glaucoma procedures. His hobbies include travel, bicycling, car restoration, the outdoors and family.

Shawn Palmer, M.D.

A former Navy flight surgeon, Dr. Palmer has cared for Folsom and Sacramento families for more than 25 years and was named to Sactown Magazine's Top Doctors in 2026.

Dr. Palmer graduated magna cum laude from Creighton University School of Medicine in 1987, in the top 1% of his class and a member of Alpha Omega Alpha. He completed general surgical residency training with the U.S. Navy, then graduated at the top of his class from the Naval Aerospace Medical Institute in Pensacola, Florida as a flight surgeon. During the Gulf War he served the Navy as a flight surgeon, ensuring naval aviators were fit to fly.

He then pursued his ophthalmology residency at the Medical College of Georgia, where as chief resident he received numerous awards and published research on tear flow that is still cited today. After practicing in a group setting he opened his own practice in 2006, and his Folsom office now anchors Eye Site Sacramento's East Bidwell Street location.

Dr. Palmer focuses on cataract surgery with the most modern options, including laser-assisted surgery to correct astigmatism and presbyopia, and manages glaucoma and diabetic retinopathy. Living in Folsom with his wife and two sons, he has coached youth sports and built friendships with thousands of patients. He enjoys hiking, skiing, cycling and travel, often with his wife's family in France.

Sina Rashidi, M.D.

Fellowship-trained in cornea and refractive surgery, Dr. Rashidi brings corneal transplantation, LASIK and PRK, and minimally invasive glaucoma surgery to the practice.

Dr. Rashidi earned dual bachelor's degrees in molecular and cell biology and psychology at the University of California, Berkeley, and his medical degree with honors from Albert Einstein College of Medicine, where he was inducted into Alpha Omega Alpha. He completed an internal medicine internship and his ophthalmology residency at the University of Louisville, where he received the Fishman Award for Excellence in Pediatric Ophthalmology, then a fellowship in cornea, external disease and refractive surgery at the University of Wisconsin, one of the nation's leading programs.

He offers advanced diagnosis and treatment of glaucoma, including the latest laser and surgical therapies and both traditional and minimally invasive glaucoma surgery. His cornea fellowship allows him to manage complex corneal disease, perform corneal transplants and provide vision correction with LASIK and PRK. He also serves as the official ophthalmologist of the Sacramento Kings.

Outside medicine he plays soccer and basketball, follows the Colts and the Pacers, collects rare sneakers, cooks, and is working his way through the New Seven Wonders of the World.

Andrew Chun, O.D.

Home-grown in Sacramento, Dr. Chun has provided full-time optometric care at the J Street office for more than a decade.

Dr. Chun received his Doctor of Optometry degree from the Southern California College of Optometry in 2007. He provides routine and diabetic eye examinations, eyeglass prescriptions and care for medical eye problems, and he co-manages patients with the surgeons when a condition needs surgical attention.

Patients describe him as skilled and exceptionally friendly. If you are due for your annual vision exam, request an appointment with Dr. Chun; you can still see any of the surgeons should the need arise.

Therapeutic optometrist · Medical eye care and dry eye

Huda Minhas, O.D.

Dr. Minhas is a therapeutic optometrist and a published clinical writer whose work on glaucoma and dry eye medications is read by optometrists nationwide.

She received an honors degree from the University of Toronto and her doctorate from the Inter-American University of Puerto Rico. During postgraduate rotations in private and ophthalmology practices she developed her skill in managing ocular disease, with a focus on diabetes, age-related macular degeneration, corneal disease and glaucoma. She specializes in myopia control and offers a range of treatment options at the Folsom office.

Passionate about education, she has written for Eyes On Eyecare on antibiotic selection, glaucoma medications, glaucoma masqueraders and dry eye therapies. Her philosophy is that healthy eyes are essential to overall health. Away from the office she enjoys time with her husband, hiking and trying new cuisines.
