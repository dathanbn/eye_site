Specialties

Routine care to advanced surgery, under one roof.

Every service below is provided by the same team. Exams, surgical consultations, pre-operative measurements and follow-up visits take place in our Sacramento and Folsom offices; surgery itself is performed at an outpatient surgery center.

01 · Cataract surgery

Cataract surgery

Drs. Jones, Serdahl, Kira, Palmer and Rashidi

A cataract is a clouding of the eye's natural lens. Surgery replaces it with a clear artificial lens, and it is one of the most common and most successful procedures in medicine.

Laser-assisted (LenSx) surgery

The LenSx femtosecond laser performs the opening of the lens capsule and the softening of the cataract with computer-guided precision before your surgeon completes the procedure. Surgery is performed at an outpatient surgery center, and most patients return to normal activities within days. Your consultation, measurements, post-operative visits and Light Adjustable Lens treatments all take place in our offices.

Advanced lens options

  • Light Adjustable Lens. The only lens that can be fine-tuned after surgery. Once your eye heals, a series of painless light treatments sets the prescription to your real-world vision.
  • Multifocal lenses. Designed to reduce dependence on glasses for both distance and near vision.
  • Toric lenses. Correct astigmatism at the time of cataract surgery.
  • Monovision. One eye set for distance and one for near, for patients who have used this approach with contact lenses.

Our eye education videos walk through each option.

02 · Glaucoma

Glaucoma

Medical, laser and surgical care

Glaucoma damages the optic nerve, usually because the pressure inside the eye is too high. It has no symptoms until vision is lost, which is why regular pressure checks matter.

We monitor eye pressure, the optic nerve and your visual field over time, and treat with prescription drops, laser treatment or surgery as needed. The goal is to keep the vision you have for the rest of your life.

03 · Laser vision correction

LASIK and PRK

Candidacy exam first

Laser vision correction reshapes the cornea to reduce or eliminate the need for glasses and contact lenses.

LASIK and PRK use the same laser but prepare the eye differently, and the right choice depends on your cornea, your prescription and your lifestyle. Every patient starts with a candidacy exam that measures all three.

04 · Comprehensive vision care

Comprehensive vision care

Drs. Chun and Minhas

Routine and diabetic eye exams, eyeglass prescriptions, and the early detection that keeps small problems small.

Our optometrists work alongside the surgeons, so if an exam finds something that needs medical attention, your care continues in the same office with the same records.

05 · Diabetic eye care

Diabetic eye care

Annual dilated exams

Diabetes can damage the small blood vessels of the retina (diabetic retinopathy) long before you notice any change in vision.

An annual dilated exam catches these changes early, when treatment is most effective. We coordinate with your primary care physician or endocrinologist so your eye care is part of your overall diabetes plan.

06 · Macular degeneration

Macular degeneration

Monitoring and treatment

Age-related macular degeneration affects the center of the retina, where sharp reading and face-recognition vision lives.

We image the macula at every visit to track changes, and treat the wet form with medication that can slow or stop vision loss. Patients with the dry form receive guidance on nutrition and monitoring at home.

07 · Dry eye therapy

Dry eye therapy

Beyond over-the-counter drops

Dry, gritty or watery eyes are among the most common complaints we see, and they are treatable.

We identify whether the problem is tear quantity, tear quality or eyelid health, and treat it with prescription drops, punctal plugs, lid therapy or a combination that fits your day.
